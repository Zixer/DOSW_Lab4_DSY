## 7. PART 4 – Story Point Estimation

### What was the biggest difficulty when estimating?
Since none of the team members have direct practical experience in developing this type of software, the main difficulty was accurately gauging the complexity of the tasks and understanding exactly what level of effort each point value represented.

### Was it easy to reach a consensus?
Yes. Since all three team members share a virtually identical knowledge base and a similar level of experience, reaching an agreement was straightforward. During discussions, we relied on technical arguments based on concepts we recalled throughout the exercise.

### How did you resolve scenarios where the estimates for the same user story were not close?
The strategy was to give the floor to the team members who submitted the most extreme values so they could explain their reasoning. After listening to and analyzing the different technical justifications and inputs, we were able to reach a constructive consensus.
